I must admit my motivation for beginning this whole experiment were entirely petty. I simply wanted to make sure my former friend suffered a little for all the small indignities he put me through. I had dabbled in some light magic before, but I deciced that I should bring out the big guns for this purpose - I wanted him to suffer humiliation and financial stress and perhaps have a few relationship mishaps, just so he would understand the level of pain I have felt. So I created a talisman and did a small invocation to charge it, hid it in his house, and everthing seemed to be going swimmingly. He missed out on a promotion; his bicycle broke down and he lost a good bit of money gambling. I was happy! But I was happy only for a moment. I wanted more pain to befall him, and I rememebred some other people who could use a few life lessons as well. And so I made more talismans and performed some more invocations. And I made one for myself to gain money and success, because who should I not be as happy as they are miserable? 

I thought I did everything correctly. I had it all written down, it was the correct time and the moon was in the right phase, but I had mixed up Tuesday and Thursday and this really did not sit well with the entities I was invoking for myself. You see, the Spirits of the Air of Thursday are for love, joy, and healing, and Tuesday's are... decicedly not that. And so, I am in a bit of a pickle. My toilet has exploded and I have developed boils, and there is a foul fog emanating from the pantry. I must undo my mistake before this goes any further.
